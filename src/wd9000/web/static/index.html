How to test

  1. Take the can out. Click Go to rest, then Go to press and check the horns' path.
  2. Adjust the sliders until the horns just push the nozzle down, then Save as press.
  3. Put the can in with the separate servo power connected.
  4. Try a Timed press of 300 ms on a covered nozzle, then outdoors.
  5. Use Hold to spray for longer bursts. It always stops after the max time.
